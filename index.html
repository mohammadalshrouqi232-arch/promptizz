    Better ideas

    Four habits that lift any prompt.

    You do not need a long idea. You need a specific one.

    Name one clear subject

    Instead ofA dragon

    TryA tired old dragon guarding a bakery oven at dawn

    Say what it is for

    Instead ofSomething for my shop

    TryA product photo for a handmade candle shop's homepage

    Use the extras

    Instead ofMake it not cheesy

    TryAvoid: clichés and stock phrases. Must include: a handwritten note

    Match length to the job

    Instead ofAlways the longest

    Rule of thumbAbout 30 to 60 words for quick tools, 100 to 150 for images and briefs, 250 or more for stories

    Troubleshooting

    If something looks off.

    The prompt is too long or short

    Check the meter under the prompt. If Fit to length appears, press it. For a bigger change, use Shorter or Longer.

    Make my prompt does nothing

    Write an idea first. If the page says it needs Claude, open it from claude.ai. Claude access has to be allowed the first time.

    My library disappeared

    Profiles live in one browser on one device. A different browser, a private window or cleared site data starts fresh. Download anything you want to keep.

    About

    A small tool with a clear job.

    Promptwright turns a rough idea into a prompt you can use anywhere, at the length and mood you choose.

    Why it exists.

    Most people know what they want to make long before they know how to ask for it. Prompts that work tend to share the same ingredients: a clear subject, a sense of style, and the right amount of detail for the tool.

    Promptwright asks for the idea, lets you set those dials, and leaves the phrasing to Claude. You can then shorten it, lengthen it, remix it or save it.

    What it does not do: it does not run your prompt in another tool, and it cannot promise what another tool will make from it. If the first result is not quite right, remix it or try a different theme.

    What sets it apart

    Built around the two things you actually tune.

    7prompt types
    12starter themes, plus your own
    500words at the long end
    5themes blended at once

    You decide the length

    Twenty words or five hundred. The meter shows the real count, and Fit to length rewrites a draft that strayed.

    Themes are open-ended

    Pick from twelve or type your own, like "1970s travel poster", and Claude weaves it in.

    Shaped for the tool

    Each type highlights what that kind of tool needs, from camera details to edge cases.

    Privacy

    In plain words.

    Sent to Claude

    Your idea and choices, only when you press Make my prompt or ask a question. Claude runs on your own account.

    Stays on this device

    Your profile, library and recent drafts sit in your browser's storage. There is no server holding them.

    Not collected

    No analytics, no ads and no tracking scripts. The only outside request is for the fonts, which come from Google Fonts.

    Accessibility

    Built to be used by anyone.

    • Everything works with a keyboard, with a clear outline on the active control.
    • Groups of choices respond to the arrow keys.
    • Animation switches off if your device asks for reduced motion.
    • Text uses high-contrast colours in both light and dark modes.
    • Changes such as new prompts and page moves are announced to screen readers.
